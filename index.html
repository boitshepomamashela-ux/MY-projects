MEET YOUR COACH

Hi, I’m Cathy.

I believe young people flourish when they feel heard.

My work is about creating that space for teens finding their footing, for young adults stepping into the world and for the parents who love them through every stage.

As an accredited NLP practitioner and life coach, I help people notice the stories they tell themselves and gently rewrite the ones holding them back. Whether I’m sitting with one person or standing in front of a full room, I show up with the same warmth and honesty.
